## Objective

**1. Introduction**

For this assignment I was directed to design and model a 3-D Truss within specific requirements.
The initial requirements were based off of this image.

<img width="388" height="247" alt="Screenshot 2026-08-25 102334" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/4a6bd51b-77c2-40ae-a37c-486fee721548" />

A is a pin support and B is a roller support. The distance a is .4 meters the distance b is .3 meters the force P is 200 Newtons. The largest internal force will be used, using a safety factor of 3.5, the pins must have a shear strength of 170 ksi and a density of 0.278 lb/in^3. Compression members are assumed not to fail by buckling for design calculations. The pins must be designed with a single shear connection, and have a safety factor of 4. The material being used is A500 Structure Steel, and the pin material is hardened tool steel. The analytical calculations will be verified using a SolidWorks 3D CAD model and mass properties.

The stress equation was used, but formed to solve for area. The highest force joint was input into the force part of the equation. While the value of A500 Structural Steel in terms of MPa was inputted for the stress value. A500 Structural Steel Grade C was chosen for the ability to withstand extreme weights while also being cost effective for the entire project. A500 Structural Steel Grade C has a Tensile Strength of 425 MPa, and a Yield Strength of 315 MPa. A500 Sctural Steel Grade C has a density of 7850 kg/m^3

A500 Structural Steel yeild strength can be found here: https://www.amerpipe.com/products/welded-pipe/a500-specifications-page/

**3. Pin Geometry**

I actually solved for my pin size first before the truss geometry as any structure is only as strong as its weakest link and I would have to base the area of the truss on how large the pin actually needs to be. In the calculations you will see both theoretical and actual calculations that are both necessary for defining minimum and maximum allowances.

Due to the fact of the largest external force being 20kN, to maintain longevity of the truss and for the pins not the be the limiting component, I rated the pins for the larger external force instead of the smaller 6.67 kN internal force max.
