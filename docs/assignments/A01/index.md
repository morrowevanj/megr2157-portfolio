**Home Page Identity**
The purpose of the homepage is to outline the functionality of the portfolio and provide those that may use the site an efficient understanding of contents and applications of the portfolio. The homepage additionally lists the standards and expectations within the portfolio. This allows users to identify necessary information and unnecessary information without going through page after page of content.
**Intentional Customization:**
The sites theme was adjusted to blue to provide contrast with the rest of the site. Similar sites like Lowes, Dell, and General Electric all use a similar blue color in their logos. This signature color aims to capture rigor and development as exemplified in the above listed companies. A color is more than a choice but an identity that reflects the rest of the product. The portfolio being the product.
**Documentation Standard:**
I will present my projects within this portfolio in a concise and efficient manner. One that presents that projects clearly and with direction.
